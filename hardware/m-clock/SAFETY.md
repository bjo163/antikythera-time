# M-Clock Safety / Trust Invariants

1. M-Clock is not currently a certified international time standard.
2. Reference lock, holdover and stale-data states must be visible on physical hardware.
3. Historical Antikythera reconstruction and M-Time digital corrections must remain separately labeled.
4. JPL/IERS/UTC are calibration/interoperability layers, not hidden dependencies of the Antikythera core.
5. Calendar/worship output must expose the profile, jurisdiction and authority source.
6. Revelation references may guide semantics and ethical constraints but may not inject unverified physical constants.
7. A failed calibration gate blocks release; thresholds must not be silently widened to hide disagreement.
