# Research Integrity Rules

The project rejects result-forcing.

1. No numerology.
2. No Qur'anic number is chosen to force an age near 13.8 Gyr.
3. Cosmological parameters are not tuned to produce a theological result.
4. Correlation is not called proof.
5. Antikythera is not claimed to contain modern cosmology.
6. Model-dependent cosmic age is not called an absolute cosmic clock.
7. Numerical, observational-parameter and model uncertainty remain separate.
8. TT/TAI/UTC/TDB/TCB are not treated as the cosmological origin.
9. References/provenance may not be fabricated.
10. Current standard cosmology remains testable and revisable.

If external evidence contradicts a hypothesis, the contradiction is a result.

Success means that declared observations + declared model + declared numerical method reproduce a checkable inference; it does not mean producing a preselected number.
