# M-Time Compatibility Policy

- MTS-2, MCLOCK-1, MOBS-1 and MAUTH-1 are stable within their major protocol IDs.
- Additive optional JSON fields are permitted.
- Removing or renaming required fields requires a new protocol ID.
- Changing instant identity/order semantics requires a new MTS major ID.
- Published Antikythera profile IDs are immutable.
- Experimental profiles retain EXPERIMENTAL in the ID until promoted through falsification and conformance gates.
