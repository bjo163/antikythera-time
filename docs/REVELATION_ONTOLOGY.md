# M-Time Revelation Temporal Ontology

Status: **TEXTUAL_REFERENCE / CONCEPTUAL**.

This layer records temporal concepts in extant textual witnesses. It does not provide numerical astronomy, metrology, eclipse, calendar-threshold, or cosmology parameters.

## Qur'an

- 2:189 — `hilal / ahillah`, `mawaqit`, people, Hajj.
- 10:5 — Sun, Moon, lunar stages/manazil, years, reckoning/hisab.
- 55:5 — Sun and Moon associated with reckoning.
- 21:33 — Sun and Moon in celestial motion/falak.

## Comparative textual witnesses

- Genesis 1:14 — luminaries, signs, appointed times/seasons, days, years.
- Psalm 104:19 — Moon and appointed times.
- Mark 13:32 — day/hour and epistemic limitation.

## Boundary

M-Time does not automatically claim that extant Torah/Psalms/Gospel textual witnesses are textually identical to the original revelation in a theological sense. That is a distinct textual/theological question.

The software invariant is stronger and simpler: every record here has no numerical scientific prior. Scientific crates are forbidden by CI from depending on `mtime-revelation`.
