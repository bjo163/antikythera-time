# Indonesia 1447 H Pilot Corpus (2026)

Purpose: first real-world replay corpus for M-Time's separation of astronomy, rukyat observation, and official authority decision.

## Ramadan 1447 H

Observation date: 17 February 2026.

Kemenag reported national hilal altitude range approximately -2°24'42" to -0°58'47" and elongation 0°56'23" to 1°53'36". The MABIMS profile was therefore not satisfied. Rukyat was conducted at 96 locations and no sighting was reported. The Government set 1 Ramadan 1447 H to 19 February 2026.

## Syawal 1447 H

Observation date: 19 March 2026.

Kemenag reported national hilal altitude range 0°54'27" to 3°07'52" and elongation 4°32'40" to 6°06'11". Even where altitude reached ~3°, the national maximum elongation remained below 6.4°, so the MABIMS profile was not satisfied. Rukyat was conducted at 117 sites with no confirmed sighting. The Government set 1 Syawal 1447 H to 21 March 2026.

## Zulhijjah 1447 H

Observation date: 17 May 2026.

Kemenag reported national altitude range 3°17'33" to 6°56'58" and elongation 8°54'49" to 10°37'07". Both MABIMS thresholds were satisfied across the stated national minima. Rukyat was conducted at 88 locations; two accepted sightings were reported in Lamongan. The Government set 1 Zulhijjah 1447 H to 18 May 2026.

## Semantic rule

The pilot MUST preserve:

`profile result != observation report != official authority decision`.

Agreement between the three layers is still represented as three independent pieces of evidence, not one merged fact.
