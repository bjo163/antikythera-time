# Cross-Jurisdiction Pilot — Shawwal 1447 H

## Purpose

This is M-Time's first source-linked cross-jurisdiction case in which official Hijri calendar outcomes differ while referring to the same lunation.

It is not used to rank or adjudicate religious methodologies.

## Indonesia

Observation date: **19 March 2026**.

The Indonesian source set reports a national Moon-altitude range of approximately 0°54'27" to 3°07'52" and elongation range 4°32'40" to 6°06'11". The national maximum elongation remains below the MABIMS 6.4° threshold. Rukyat was organized at 117 sites with no confirmed sighting. The Government set **1 Shawwal 1447 H = 21 March 2026**.

## Türkiye / Diyanet

Diyanet publishes conjunction at **19 March 2026 01:24 UTC** and first visibility at **15:24 UTC**. Its Shawwal page reports Moon altitude at sunset of approximately 6°40' in Ankara and 5°58' in Mecca and states that its visibility criteria were satisfied. A later official release reports observations at 111 locations and that the crescent was observed and photographed in North America. Diyanet's official calendar places **1 Shawwal 1447 H = 20 March 2026**.

## What M-Time may conclude

The published **calendar outcome differs by one civil day**.

The documented process also differs in calendar methodology/profile, observation scope/evidence, jurisdiction and authority.

M-Time must **not** collapse this into "astronomy A versus astronomy B." Local observer states differ geographically, and Diyanet's current published methodology contains additional global/regional timing conditions beyond the 5°/8° visibility component.

## What M-Time does not conclude

M-Time does not decide which fiqh methodology is correct, preferred, or religiously binding.

The purpose is narrower and testable:

> preserve enough provenance that the reason for a differing calendar outcome can be inspected instead of being hidden inside a date string.
