## External work after v2

The remaining path is organizational/scientific rather than an internal version milestone:

1. unaffiliated third-party implementations;
2. peer review of the preprint;
3. independent audit of reference datasets/thresholds;
4. longer operational use and compatibility reports;
5. discussion with relevant astronomy/time standards communities;
6. any formal adoption process chosen by those communities.

## Permanent scientific rule

Every layer states what is measured, conventional, modeled/inferred, reconstructed/speculative, uncertain, and externally falsifiable.
