# Qur'anic Celestial Computation Map

**Status:** TEXTUAL / CONCEPTUAL RESEARCH LAYER.

This document is not numerical calibration data. No verse supplies H0, Ωm, ΩΛ, w0, wa or a Big-Bang date.

## 55:5 — حسبان / ḥusbān

Arabic phrase: `الشمس والقمر بحسبان`.

Classical tafsir such as al-Tabari and Ibn Kathir discusses the Sun and Moon as proceeding by reckoning/calculation and appointed courses. The root ح س ب is associated with counting/reckoning/calculation.

Conceptual state:

```text
celestial regularity -> HISAB / reckoning
```

Machine label: `TEXTUAL_REFERENCE`.

## 10:5 — lunar stages, years and reckoning

The verse links the Sun, Moon, lunar stations (`منازل`) and knowing the number of years and calculation (`الحساب`). Classical and modern tafsir commonly connect the stations with calendrical reckoning.

```text
astronomical observation -> phase/stage -> chronology -> calculation
```

## 21:33 and 36:38-40 — فلك / falak

21:33 states that night/day, Sun and Moon are created and that each moves in a `falak`. Classical tafsir explains `falak` using a circular/rounded course or domain of motion; interpretations of the physical cosmology must not be projected backward uncritically.

## 51:47 — لموسعون / lamūsiʿūn

Interpretation is separated into categories.

### TEXT
The Arabic includes `وَإِنَّا لَمُوسِعُونَ`.

### CLASSICAL TAFSIR
Ibn Kathir explains God's power and making the heaven vast/wide. Classical commentary is not a numerical Hubble-law derivation.

### MODERN COSMOLOGICAL INTERPRETATION
Some modern translations/commentaries render the phrase in a way that can be correlated conceptually with an expanding universe. This project labels that relationship:

`CONCEPTUAL_CORRELATION`

not:

`SCIENTIFIC_PARAMETER`.

No H0 is derived from the verse.

## 54:49 and 67:3-4
