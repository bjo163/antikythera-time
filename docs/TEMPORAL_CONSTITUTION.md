# M-Time Temporal Constitution

These invariants govern the Phase 1–5 architecture.

1. **Antikythera is the computational core concept.** Modern ephemerides calibrate it; they do not silently replace it.
2. **Linear time and cyclic time coexist.** Neither is silently collapsed into the other.
3. **Historical reconstruction and modern digital correction are separately labeled.**
4. **Physical measurement is not altered by theology.**
5. **Revelation may guide temporal semantics and ethics, but may not manufacture empirical constants.**
6. **Astronomy is separate from calendar criteria.**
7. **Calendar criteria are separate from observation.**
8. **Observation is separate from jurisdiction and authority.**
9. **Every external datum should carry provenance, version and integrity information where available.**
10. **Every major quantitative claim must be falsifiable by an independent reference or implementation.**
11. **Unknown evidence remains unknown; it is not converted to a pass.**
12. **Calibration failures are investigated before thresholds are changed.**

This constitution is architectural, not a claim of religious or standards authority.
