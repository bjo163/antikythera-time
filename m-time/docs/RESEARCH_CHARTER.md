# M-Time Research Charter

1. SI second remains the physical duration foundation.
2. Time scale, frame, observer, uncertainty and provenance are explicit.
3. Astronomy never silently becomes a religious/legal ruling.
4. A calendar profile is a versioned rule, not a physical fact.
5. Rukyat observation is distinct from hisab/calculation.
6. Authority decisions are normative records, not astronomical measurements.
7. Antikythera is computational inspiration/history; reconstruction claims carry evidence status.
8. Cosmic age is model-dependent inference, never an absolute timestamp.
9. Revelation texts are TEXTUAL_REFERENCE / CONCEPTUAL; they never inject numerical physics parameters without independent scientific derivation.
10. A failed external-reference test is a result, not something to tune away.

## Flagship acceptance criterion

Given two Hijri-resolution records, M-Time identifies which layer(s) differ: physical input, ephemeris, observer, criterion, observation, jurisdiction, authority, or source version.
